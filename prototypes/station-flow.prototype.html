RAWForge — station flow

A throwaway prototype for issue #10. Not production code.

The question this answers A station is a pose. One station may hold several capture sets across the three rear sensors, and swapping sensor means reconfiguring the capture session — a visible pause with the photographer holding still. Does that model survive the awkward cases: a bracket that fails its clipping check, a station disturbed halfway through, the app being killed mid-capture, a single-frame set that is not a bracket at all, and a shot list authored in an order that costs extra swaps? Press the buttons and watch the state and the event log.
