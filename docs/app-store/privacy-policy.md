# RAWForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-01

RAWForge is an on-device Bayer RAW camera and capture-recording tool. It does not require or provide an account. The app has no backend, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs, and it does not send data to RAWForge or to third parties.

## Permissions

RAWForge uses the Camera to show a live preview and save the RAW captures you choose to make. Camera access is required for capture.

RAWForge can use Motion during a capture to record how steadily the iPhone was held. Motion access is optional. If Motion is unavailable or denied, capture can continue without motion evidence and the capture record reflects that no motion stream was available.

RAWForge does not request access to the Photos library.

## Data stored on this iPhone

RAWForge stores user-created data locally in its Documents directory. This can include DNG frames, capture and sensor records, motion samples and summaries, Recipes, and bounded diagnostic log files. Files that support the app's operation, such as the current shot list, device profile, and privacy-migration state, are stored locally in Application Support.

Capture records can contain capture dates, exposure and focus settings, detected camera capabilities, device and app build information, motion evidence, and error or completion details. RAW image content remains in the DNG files on the iPhone unless you export it.

## Export and transmission

RAWForge does not automatically transmit any capture, diagnostic, or device data. Exports happen only when you initiate them through the iOS share sheet, the Files app, or a cable connection and choose a destination. A copy sent to a destination you choose is then governed by that destination's privacy practices; RAWForge cannot delete or control exported copies.

## Deletion and retention

You can delete a complete capture session in RAWForge. This removes that session's local directory, including its DNG frames and records. RAWForge also removes frames from an incomplete capture that is abandoned or fails before it can be banked. Recipes can be deleted separately without deleting completed capture sessions.

Diagnostic logs are kept locally for a bounded number of recent launches, with older launch logs removed automatically. Deleting RAWForge removes data remaining in the app's container under normal iOS behavior. Deletion does not remove copies that you previously exported.

## iCloud backup

RAWForge marks capture-session folders, including their DNG frames and session metadata, as excluded from iCloud backup. Small operational files in Application Support remain subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

## Diagnostics

Local diagnostics can contain the RAWForge version, build and source commit; iPhone model and iOS version; detected camera capabilities; capture settings, progress, and outcomes; storage, battery, and thermal status; motion summaries; filenames or session identifiers; faults; and error messages. Durable diagnostics use relative elapsed times and do not contain raw system boot time. They do not contain RAW image bytes, image previews, or analytics identifiers.

Diagnostics remain on the iPhone unless you explicitly share a diagnostic file. RAWForge does not receive diagnostic files automatically.

## Analytics and tracking

RAWForge does not use analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, tracking domains, or identifiers for tracking. It does not sell or share personal data.

## Contact

Privacy questions and reports can be submitted through the public RAWForge issue tracker: https://github.com/tangericm/RAWForge/issues
